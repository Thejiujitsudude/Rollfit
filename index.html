How it works
1. Open the app. Today's workout is ready.
2. Lift what it tells you. Weights are loaded for you. Tap each set when it's done.
3. It adjusts as you get stronger. Easy sets push the weight up. Earn stripes and belts along the way.
Why Roll Fit
Built for grapplers, not bodybuilders. Every session trains what you use on the mat: hip drive, pulling, grip, and a strong trunk.
Lifts chosen for jiu-jitsu. Trap bar deadlift, Zercher squat and dumbbell bench build real strength while going easier on the back and shoulders that rolling beats up.
Fits around your training. Rolled today? Flip one switch and your lift gets trimmed so you're not wrecked for class.
No guesswork. A 9-week plan builds up, backs off, then tests your new max, and sets your weights for you.
